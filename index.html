01 — About

From accounting ledgers to neural networks.

I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Tufts University, where I also work as a Research and Teaching Assistant. My path here wasn't linear — I started in accounting, moved through project management, crash-data analysis, and health informatics, before landing on the question I care about most: how deep learning can help us understand protein behavior. Today my research focuses on protein solubility prediction and protein–protein interaction (PPI) modeling, using deep learning to turn biological complexity into something computable.

02 — Research Focus

What I'm working on.

🧬

Protein Solubility Prediction

Developing deep learning models to predict protein solubility directly from sequence and structural features — work that feeds into faster, cheaper decisions in protein engineering and drug development pipelines.

🔗

Protein–Protein Interaction (PPI)

Building neural network approaches to model and predict PPIs, with a focus on architectures that generalize across unseen protein pairs and scale to large interaction datasets.

📊

Applied ML for Biology

Grounded in a health informatics background, I'm interested in the full pipeline — from messy biological data to models that are actually useful to researchers and clinicians.
